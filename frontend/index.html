    One place to check every pharmacy

    Finding a medicine in Kigali often means walking or riding from one pharmacy to the next, asking the same question at each counter. PharmaLink answers it before you set off.

    We connect to the stock systems pharmacies already use, so what you see here is what they have recorded on their own shelves: which medicines are in, what they cost, and when that information last changed.

    Pharmacies keep their own system. Joining PharmaLink does not mean replacing software or retraining staff. We read the stock list a pharmacy already keeps and show it to people nearby who are looking for those medicines.

    1. Search for the medicine

      Type the name on the box or the generic name. Both work.

    2. Compare price and distance

      See every pharmacy that has it, what each one charges, and how far each one is from you.

    3. Go and buy it there

      Get directions or call ahead. You pay at the pharmacy; for prescription medicines, take your prescription with you.
